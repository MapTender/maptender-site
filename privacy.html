Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

This policy explains what information MapTender collects and how we use it. MapTender is run by Kwaday Corppetts, a sole proprietor doing business as MapTender.

What we collect

  • Your name and business name
  • Your email address and phone number
  • Access to your Google Business Profile that you choose to grant us
  • Anything else you send us when you email, call or text

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card details. Stripe's own privacy policy covers the payment information you give them.

How we use it

We use your information only to provide our service: to work on your Google listing, contact you about it, send your monthly report and handle billing.

What we don't do

We don't sell, rent or trade your information. We only share it with the tools we need to run the service (such as Stripe for payments and our email provider), or if the law requires it.

Your Google listing access

You can remove our access to your Google Business Profile at any time from your Google account. When you cancel, we'll stop using it.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing hello@maptender.com. We may keep billing records we're required to keep.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
